The dual-stack approach

WHY BOTH
DISCIPLINES.

I came up through the Rugbones Collective, producing and beat-making, and I still work hand in hand with them — a partnership that will never die. So I picked up live sound too, and cut our setup time in half. That is the pattern: whatever is slowing the work down becomes the next thing I learn.

Diploma in Music Production from ADMI (2023–24), now finishing a Higher Diploma in Music Production & Sound Engineering. Freelance engineer at Sub Sahara Records, credits with Skrilla, Nuclear Ke and Karis — and a self-taught developer on the side.
